# CalcRun Comprehensive Review — 2 July 2026

Produced by a 15-agent review across 10 dimensions (technical SEO on the live site, content
strategy, visual design, homepage conversion, calculator UX, code quality, performance,
monetization, prior-audit synthesis, competitor benchmark). Critical/high factual findings were
independently re-verified by adversarial checker agents; verified findings are marked
[CONFIRMED]. ~120 findings total; this document contains the synthesis and the proposed plan.

---

## Executive Summary

The site is in far better shape than its results suggest — and in far worse shape than its
dashboards claim. The product is top-quartile versus calculator.net/Bankrate on UX (live
sliders, dark mode, currency toggle, interactive homepage hero — none of the incumbents have
these). The code is decently engineered (232 passing unit tests, disciplined error handling,
proper code-splitting). Technical SEO fundamentals (canonicals, sitemap, robots, redirects) are
clean.

But four structural failures make the business a zero right now:

1. **Distribution never happened.** Zero backlinks, zero third-party mentions anywhere on the
   web. The launch plan (Product Hunt, Reddit, HN, Dev.to — all written since March) was never
   executed. Google indexes ~7 of 196 pages and hasn't recrawled some pages in 4+ months.
   No on-site fix can overcome this. [CONFIRMED]
2. **The core trust claim is now false.** Every tax page displays "Updated for 2025/26 tax
   year" — but the UK 2026/27 tax year started 6 April 2026. US rates are 2025 pre-OBBBA
   (standard deduction wrong even for 2025). Scottish bands appear to be 2024/25 values. The
   repo has had zero commits since 20 March. [CONFIRMED]
3. **Revenue is structurally $0.** Of ~230 affiliate placement slots, exactly 2 point to
   partners that pay (NordPass/NordVPN — both quarantined on the password-generator and QR-code
   pages). All finance placements (Betterment x47, InvestEngine x33, LendingTree x31...) are
   untracked links that earn nothing per click. The MailerLite drip was never activated, so
   every captured email receives nothing, forever. Ally (declined by CJ in March) still occupies
   6 placements. [CONFIRMED]
4. **~70 of 102 scenario pages are numeric-variant doorway pages** (44-53% content overlap
   between neighbors, measured) — the classic trigger for "Discovered — currently not indexed"
   at scale, dragging sitewide quality down. Plus 8-9 scenario/comparison pairs target
   identical keywords (three with byte-identical slugs). [CONFIRMED]

On the "professional, super modern, sleek" goal: the design reviewers' verdict is that the site
currently reads as "a high-quality AI-generated content site, not a premium product." The tells
are specific and fixable: positive money rendered in coral-red, gradient-text numbers and
gradient dividers, dated body-serif used as display type, default recharts output, 28px slider
thumbs, badge/pill soup, interior pages wearing a different (navy-hero) brand than the homepage,
a sitemap-dump below the fold, and 5 marketing modules per calculator page on a site whose
tagline is "no ads."

## Findings by Theme (condensed)

### A. Why only ~7 pages are indexed (diagnosis, ranked)

1. **Zero off-page authority** — no backlinks, no mentions; Google has deprioritized the
   domain. Stale SERP snippets prove no recrawl in 4+ months. Primary cause. [CONFIRMED]
2. **Doorway-page pattern** — ~70 numeric-variant scenarios, 44-53% 5-gram overlap between
   neighbors. Matches Google's scaled-content/doorway policies. [CONFIRMED]
3. **Every internal link 307-redirects** — all 166 unique internal hrefs omit the trailing
   slash; canonical/sitemap use trailing slash; the Worker 307s every hop. Doubled crawl cost,
   no permanence signal. [CONFIRMED]
4. **23 methodology pages are complete orphans** — zero internal links to the site's best
   E-E-A-T asset. [CONFIRMED]
5. **8-9 cannibal scenario/comparison pairs**, three with identical slugs. [CONFIRMED]
6. Zero E-E-A-T markup on all 117 scenario/comparison pages: no author, no dates, no Article
   schema, no sources. The ACA credential appears once, first-name-only, on /about. Competitors
   that rank (Omni, TheCalculatorSite, SmartAsset) all ship named bylines + reviewer + dates on
   the calculator page itself. [CONFIRMED]
7. Supporting issues: no sitemap lastmod, duplicate BreadcrumbList blocks, meta descriptions
   missing primary keywords (Sprint 17 over-corrected), finance topical authority diluted by 20
   dev/file tools in the same sitemap.

Also verified clean: robots.txt, sitemap coverage, canonicals, bare-domain 301, no noindex, no
X-Robots-Tag, 404s correct, OG images resolve. The March "4 redirect errors" should already be
fixed by the Cloudflare rule (needs GSC validation click).

**Strategic overlay:** comparison "X vs Y" queries trigger AI Overviews ~95% of the time and
informational pages have lost 30-40% of clicks — while interactive calculators are the least
AIO-disrupted format (<3%). The defensible asset is the tools; scenarios/comparisons should be
consolidated into fewer, richer, monetizable pages, not scaled.

### B. Why it doesn't feel "professional, super modern, sleek" (design review)

The reviewers converged on a specific list of tells:

- **Semantic color error:** take-home pay and interest earned — the numbers users most want to
  grow — render in coral-red (`text-accent-600`). Red means loss on a finance site. Meanwhile
  the same coral is used for genuine costs elsewhere, so the color means nothing.
- **AI-slop fingerprints:** navy→teal gradient text on hero numbers, teal→coral gradient on
  stat numbers, gradient hairlines on every h2, gradient hr, icon-in-teal-rounded-square chips
  repeated everywhere (all 9 comparison cards share the same scales icon), aurora radial glows.
- **Typography:** Libre Baskerville is a 2012 body serif used as display type — reads
  dated-blog, not premium. Serif/sans usage is inconsistent across card types.
  design-system.md still says Inter.
- **Charts look default-recharts:** dashed 3-3 grid, default legend dots, two near-identical
  green/teal series on the flagship chart, off-brand amber donut on mortgage, per-series
  colored tooltip text.
- **Brand incoherence:** homepage is all off-white; every interior index/scenario/methodology
  page opens with a 260-330px mostly-empty dark navy band. Two different sites.
- **Component-level:** 28px slider thumb overhanging an 8px track on the core interaction;
  5+ distinct pill/badge treatments per page; 2015-Bootstrap affiliate cards with three
  identical "Get Started Free" block buttons; hand-traced logo wordmark with wobbly letterform
  contours; cookie banner floating over content on every page.
- **Layout:** below-the-fold homepage is three columns of plain text links (a sitemap dump);
  calculator prose sits in a 770px column leaving a dead right third; newsletter card floats
  tiny in an empty band.
- **Marketing module overload:** disclosure banner + inline affiliate card wedged between
  action buttons and chart + email box + 3-card affiliate box + scroll email bar = 5 modules
  per calculator page. The single biggest premium-feel killer.
- **Dark mode:** real but fragile — ~260 lines of per-class overrides; chart legend barely
  legible; teal buttons muddy on dark cards.
- **Mobile:** results are two screens below the inputs — the real-time USP is invisible at the
  moment of interaction. Cookie banner + scroll email bar + back-to-top FAB all fight for the
  bottom edge.

### C. Performance

- All hashed assets and fonts served with `Cache-Control: max-age=0, must-revalidate` —
  repeat visits revalidate ~38 files. One-line _headers fix. [CONFIRMED]
- React (~62KB gz) ships on all 197 pages solely to hydrate the cookie banner. ~150 pure
  content pages should ship ~0KB JS. [CONFIRMED]
- Calculator pages eagerly load ~600KB raw / 194KB gz at idle; recharts is inside a 301KB
  shared chunk statically imported by every calculator. SSR makes inputs LOOK ready while dead
  for 2-4s on mid-range mobile. No modulepreload hints. [CONFIRMED]
- Homepage hero is the site's only `client:load` island. [CONFIRMED]
- 40-70KB of every page is duplicated inline nav SVG icons (44 tool icons x2 per page).
- Turnstile third-party script loads on every calculator page even if the email form is never
  opened. GA4 script is the first element in head, ahead of charset/CSS/font preloads.
- Quick wins: Astro prefetch not enabled; 4 orphaned TTFs shipped; JetBrains Mono unsubsetted
  (92KB vs ~28KB).

### D. Monetization

- 99% of placements → non-paying partners; only Nord links pay, on 2 utility pages. [CONFIRMED]
- MailerLite drip never activated — subscribers get nothing. Templates are fully written in
  docs/mailerlite-drip-setup.md; needs ~2h of owner UI work. [CONFIRMED]
- Partner URLs duplicated in 3 places (affiliate-data.ts, ResultAffiliate.tsx, worker.ts);
  when a tracked link lands it must be updated in all 3 or revenue silently leaks. worker.ts
  would also corrupt a CJ tracked URL by appending UTM params. [CONFIRMED]
- All 35 salary scenarios — the flagship long-tail content — have zero monetization AND zero
  email capture. [CONFIRMED]
- Ally (declined) still on 6 placements + disclosure page. [CONFIRMED]
- Per-page EPC not computable: no SubID on tracked links; GA4 custom dimensions
  (partner/placement/page_type) almost certainly never registered in GA4 Admin. [CONFIRMED]
- Results emails label plain marketing URLs as "affiliate links" — a disclosure claim that is
  currently untrue. [CONFIRMED]
- FTC: scenario/comparison pages show disclosure only BELOW the affiliate cards.
- Economics: affiliate-only needs ~50-100k finance sessions/mo for $500-1,000/mo. Concentration
  risk: Betterment holds 47+ slots riding on one pending application.

## Design Direction (for the "super modern and sleek" goal)

Two candidate directions were developed; recommendation is **B**.

**A. "Editorial Ledger"** (evolve current identity, ~lower risk): Fraunces (variable, display
optical sizing) replaces Libre Baskerville for H1/H2 only; body stays DM Sans; kill all
gradient text/dividers; teal only on interactive elements; coral only on one CTA per page;
compact light interior headers. Same brand, executed like a serious editorial-finance product.

**B. "Precision Instrument" (RECOMMENDED)**: numbers-first product aesthetic in the
Linear/Mercury/Ramp register. One grotesk (Inter Tight or Space Grotesk) for everything;
**JetBrains Mono (already self-hosted) for every financial figure, table column, axis tick and
result number — mono numerals become the brand signature**. Near-monochrome ink/paper palette
(ink #0E1116 / paper #FCFCFA) with deep teal as the sole accent; 1px borders replace drop
shadows; 6-8px radii; ledger-style dense results tables; flat custom charts (2px teal line +
neutral-400 comparison, direct end-labels, no legend, annotated crossover markers); compact
light page headers everywhere; re-set logo wordmark in the brand face. Rationale: the product
IS numbers — monospace precision typography is the one move that makes a calculator site
instantly feel engineered rather than templated, it eliminates every identified slop tell
wholesale, costs $0 in fonts, and ships component-by-component (tokens → SliderInput → chart
theme → cards/badges → headers) without a rebuild.

Chart storytelling upgrades ride along: annotated "interest overtakes your contributions in
year 14" marker on compound interest, principal/interest crossover on mortgage, donut center
KPIs ("78% kept"), 300ms count-up on headline numbers.

## THE PLAN

Structure: 5 code phases (Claude) + 1 owner track (manual, runs in parallel from day 1).
Every phase ends with build + full test suite + fresh screenshots; design phases end with
before/after screenshots for your sign-off.

### Phase 1 — Correctness & Trust Emergency (~1-2 sessions)

The trust claim must be true before anything else matters.

1. Research + update tax data with cited sources: UK 2026/27 (income tax, NI, Scottish bands,
   student loan Plans 1/2/4/5 incl. first Plan 5 repayments, state pension triple-lock),
   US 2026 (brackets, SS wage base, post-OBBBA standard deduction — and correct 2025 values).
2. Make Stamp Duty + CGT calculators import from the central rates modules (delete inline
   tables); verify SDLT/CGT rates current.
3. Derive all tax-year badges/copy from the rate constants (currently hardcoded in 3+ places,
   including a UK-format year on the US page); wire scripts/check-freshness.ts into the build.
4. Recalculate all salary scenario figures + titles to 2026/27 / 2026 (mechanical pass;
   deeper rewrite happens in Phase 3).
5. Fix verified bugs: debt payoff rollover (+ pin tests + re-verify the snowball/avalanche
   article numbers), solar slider no-op, keyboard-inoperable sliders, stale clamp-error
   message, pension access age + IHT-2027 caveat, accuracy audit of all 15 comparisons.
6. Extract the 4 new calculators' math into src/lib + unit tests with HMRC/IRS hand-verified
   examples; add them to the E2E suites.
7. Consent fix: GA4 via Consent Mode v2 default-denied (keeps measurement, makes the banner
   honest), slim non-overlapping banner. [Decision 3 below]
8. Worker hardening: fail-closed Turnstile, surface MailerLite failures, add Turnstile to
   /api/subscribe.
9. Affiliate hygiene: remove Ally everywhere, per-partner CTA copy (kill blanket "Get Started
   Free"), stop labeling untracked email links as affiliate links, disclosure above cards on
   scenario/comparison pages.
10. Cheap technical wins bundled here: trailing-slash everywhere + `trailingSlash: 'always'`
    (kills the 166 x 307-redirect problem), link methodology pages from calculators + footer,
    cache headers for /_astro and /fonts, delete orphaned TTFs + dead deps (docx, mammoth,
    html2canvas, html2pdf.js), fix npm run check heap, update CLAUDE.md.

### Phase 2 — Design Overhaul: "Precision Instrument" (~3-4 sessions) [Decision 1]

Sequenced so each step ships independently:

1. **Tokens:** ink/paper palette, teal-only accent, semantic tokens (bg-surface/text-ink/
   border-line) — this also replaces the fragile 260-line dark-mode override list.
2. **Typography:** single grotesk + JetBrains Mono (subsetted, ~28KB) for all numerals;
   real type scale; semantic number colors (gains=ink or green, costs=red, never coral
   decoration).
3. **Core components:** SliderInput (16-18px thumb, 4-6px track, no overhang, value bubble,
   keyboard fix from Phase 1), one badge component (2 variants max), buttons, 1px-border
   cards, quiet-recommendation affiliate rows, re-set logo wordmark.
4. **Chart system:** shared chartTheme module all 23 calculators inherit — flat grid, direct
   labels, brand series colors, neutral tooltips, donut center KPIs, annotated crossover
   markers, count-up headline numbers, dark-mode-aware.
5. **Page chrome:** compact light interior headers replace the empty navy bands; solid
   hairlines replace gradient dividers; kill gradient text; prose column gets a sticky TOC.
6. **Calculator page recomposition:** marketing module diet (ONE email capture per page;
   ResultAffiliate moves below the schedule table; trim affiliate box to live/relevant
   partners), sticky summary-only results header (fixes the nested-scrollbar trap), mobile
   sticky result mini-bar (makes the real-time USP visible on phones), fix the ragged mobile
   button row.
7. **Homepage:** restyle hero to the new system; replace the Solar tab with "What's my
   take-home pay?" (UK + US — restores the regressed UK prompt); make "Open full calculator"
   the solid primary CTA; give tabs clickable affordance; below-fold becomes designed rows
   with pre-computed result previews in mono numerals ("£50K salary → £3,101/mo take-home"),
   a trust strip (ACA credential + "every calculator shows its formula" → methodology links +
   tax-year verification), a differentiator proof strip, visible search, honest newsletter
   card.
8. **During the migration, introduce CalculatorShell** (shared button row / sticky layout /
   PDF sections / getResults wiring) and migrate calculators onto it as they're restyled —
   the design pass is the natural moment to kill the 52.7% duplication.
9. Performance rides along: CookieConsent → vanilla Astro script (drops React from ~150
   pages), hero → client:idle, recharts → React.lazy behind the fixed-height container with
   shimmer, Turnstile deferred to form-open, GA4 to end of head, nav SVG sprite, Astro
   prefetch, modulepreload hints.

### Phase 3 — Content & SEO Restructure (~2-3 sessions) [gated on owner GSC pull; Decision 2]

1. Merge the 8-9 cannibal scenario/comparison pairs (keep comparison, 301 the twin).
2. Consolidate the ~70 doorway scenarios into interactive hub pages ("UK take-home pay by
   salary" table page with anchors + embedded calculator; US equivalent; "mortgage payments
   by amount" hub) with 301s from the variants. Keep ~30 genuinely differentiated scenarios.
3. E-E-A-T hardening on everything that survives: byline block (full name, ACA, photo,
   link to expanded author page with ICAEW/LinkedIn footprint), datePublished/dateModified in
   schemas and rendered, Article + FAQPage structured data, sources on comparisons, dedupe
   breadcrumb schema, sitemap lastmod.
4. Internal linking: nearest-neighbor related scenarios (salary ladders), in-body links in
   comparisons, tool → scenario cluster links.
5. Meta rewrites: keyword-leading tool descriptions, expand the 18 stubby utility
   descriptions.
6. 5-8 NEW hand-written high-intent pages targeting the verified gaps: pay-rise shock (UK+US),
   60% tax trap, £100k childcare cliff, Plan 5 student loan explainer, stamp duty FTB 2026,
   PCP-vs-HP-vs-loan, CGT on a second home 2026/27 — all with byline/schema/calculator links.
   (These replace the March Sprint 34 list, updated for what's now saturated.)
7. Consider sitemap-excluding the 20 dev/file tools (keep pages live). [Decision 5]

### Phase 4 — Revenue Plumbing (~1 session)

3. Salary scenarios + hubs get email capture; NordPass/NordVPN extended to the dev-tool pages

5. Prep pack for the owner's affiliate approvals: exact URL swap locations, per-partner test
