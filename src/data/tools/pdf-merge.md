## Why Merge PDFs?

Sending multiple PDF attachments is messy — recipients get confused by file order, email clients may block large numbers of attachments, and it looks unprofessional. A single merged PDF is cleaner, easier to navigate, and simpler to archive.

## How It Works

1. Upload or drag multiple PDF files into the tool
2. Reorder them using the arrow buttons
3. Click "Merge & Download" to get a single combined PDF

The entire process uses your browser's built-in capabilities. No data leaves your computer.

## Tips for Better Results

- **Name your files clearly** before uploading so you can easily identify them in the merge order.
- **Check page count** — the tool shows how many pages each PDF has, so you can verify nothing is missing.
- **Large files**: For PDFs over 50MB each, processing may take a few seconds. Be patient and don't refresh.
